Help

Tapiroute moves a pawn along a real road with the kilometres you walk on your treadmill.

Which treadmills work with Tapiroute?

Connected treadmills that speak Bluetooth FTMS, the standard for connected treadmills, such as the Zipro Luma. Put your phone near the treadmill, switched on: Tapiroute finds it by itself. Tapiroute reads speed and distance, it never controls the treadmill.

My treadmill is not found

  • Check that Bluetooth is on and that Tapiroute may use it (iOS Settings > Tapiroute).
  • Close other apps connected to the treadmill: it only talks to one at a time.
  • Switch the treadmill off and on again, and bring the phone closer.

I walked without my phone

Add the session by hand: Profile > My sessions > Add a session. It counts for your journeys, but not in races.

The distance differs from the treadmill's

In My account > Manage my treadmills, run the speed calibration again: Tapiroute compares what the treadmill shows with what it receives over Bluetooth.

Delete my account

My account > Delete my account. Everything is erased at once and for good. To keep a copy first, use Export my data. Details are in the privacy policy.
