Privacy

Updated on 15 November 2026

Cap is an open source app that moves a pawn along a real road with the distance you walk on a treadmill. This page says which data Cap keeps, why, and how to erase it.

What Cap does not do

  • Cap never knows where you are: no location tracking, only the start and destination addresses you type.
  • No ads, no analytics tools, no data sold.

The data kept

  • Account: first name, username, e-mail address and hashed password (Argon2), or the identifier given by Sign in with Apple. Country and city if you give them: the city is never shown to others.
  • Profile photo, if you add one.
  • Sessions: distance, duration, speed, incline and steps, read from your treadmill over Bluetooth.
  • Journeys: the start and destination addresses, the stages and your progress.
  • Community: friends and follows, race invitations, posts, comments, Bravos, published routes with their photos, reports and badges.

This data is only used to run Cap: saving your progress, showing rankings and the feed, and running races between friends.

Who sees what

Your friends and the walkers who follow you see your first name, photo, kilometres and posts, depending on the visibility chosen in My account (“Profile visible to”). Routes you publish are visible to everyone.

Where the data lives

On a server hosted in France by Cap's author, reached over HTTPS only. Routing and address search run on that same server. Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap and satellite views from IGN (Géoplateforme): these services see your device's IP address, like any website, but receive no account data.

How long

As long as your account exists. Sessions not yet uploaded also stay on your phone.

Your rights

  • Export all your data: My account > Export my data.
  • Delete your account and all its data, at once and for good: My account > Delete my account.
  • You can also correct your details from your profile, and contact the CNIL (the French data protection authority) if needed.
